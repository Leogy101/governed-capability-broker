# Apache-2.0 licensing and attribution

The owner has approved **Apache License 2.0** for GCB. The adopted [LICENSE](../../LICENSE) contains the license text, and [NOTICE](../../NOTICE) records:

> Copyright 2026 Brandon Gordon and GCB contributors.

Apache-2.0 permits independent tools, integrations and commercial embedding under its terms. Its explicit contributor patent grant and notice requirements fit this project. The license governs GCB's original source, fixtures and documentation; see the [official license](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) for the complete terms.

Package metadata declares `Apache-2.0` and includes `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` in the built distribution. Contributors must have the right to license their work and preserve required third-party notices. No CLA service or dual-licensing scheme is introduced.

[Third-party attribution](../../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) identifies build tools, external runtimes and model-weight boundaries. No model weights, provider credentials, subscription rights or product trademarks are relicensed by GCB. Dependencies and external services retain their own terms.

Licensing and the clean-snapshot strategy are approved. **Public repository creation, remote pushes, a GitHub prerelease and package publication are not yet authorized.** The [release plan](RELEASE_PLAN.md) defines the remaining explicit publication gate.
